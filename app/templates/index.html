Why early screening matters

Stigma, limited awareness and long waiting times mean many cases of PTSD are identified only once symptoms are severe. Earlier identification improves recovery.

{{ icon('activity') }}

Evidence-based risk factors

Combat exposure, deployment length, prior trauma, sleep, anxiety (GAD-7), depression (PHQ-9), alcohol use (AUDIT) and social isolation.

{{ icon('eye') }}

Transparent, not a black box

Logistic Regression shows exactly which answers raised or lowered each estimate, so clinicians can check and trust it.

{{ icon('server') }}

Built for low-resource settings

Runs on modest hardware and slow connections: no heavy frameworks, no external services.

{{ icon('lock') }}

Confidential by design

Role-based access, hashed passwords, consent before every assessment, and a full audit trail of who viewed what.

How it works

  1. Register & sign in

    Create your account with your service number.

  2. Complete the assessment

    Ten questions about your deployment and wellbeing, after giving consent.

  3. See your risk level

    Low, Moderate or High, with the factors behind it and next steps.

  4. Get support

    A mental health professional reviews results and follows up. Reassess over time to track progress.

For clinicians and administrators

Mental health professionals see a prioritised caseload, highest risk first, with each person's history and a place for clinical notes. Administrators manage accounts, assessment wording, model versions and the audit log.
