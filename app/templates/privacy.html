Privacy & data protection

How this system handles your information.

What we collect

Your name, email, service number and optional rank and branch; your answers to each assessment; the risk estimate produced; notes written by mental health professionals; and a log of actions such as sign-ins and who viewed an assessment.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own assessments and results.
  • Authorised mental health professionals can see assessments in order to provide support. Every view is recorded.
  • Administrators manage accounts and the system, but cannot open assessment answers.

How it is protected

  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never in readable form.
  • Access is controlled by role and checked on the server for every page.
  • Sessions time out after 30 minutes of inactivity; repeated failed sign-ins lock the account temporarily.
  • Connections are encrypted (HTTPS) in production.

Consent

You are asked for informed consent before each assessment. Taking part is voluntary.

Important

This system provides decision-support only, not a medical diagnosis. If you are in danger, call {{ support_line }}.

Data protection in Kenya is governed by the Data Protection Act, 2019. Operational deployment requires approval by the organisation's data protection officer.
